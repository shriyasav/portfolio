How I think about data

A point of view

01

Data without narrative is noise

Numbers alone don't move organisations. The real craft is translating complex datasets into stories that make a CFO act, a CMO shift budget, and an engineering team prioritise. Storytelling is the most underrated analytical skill.

02

Governance is a growth engine

Most companies treat data governance as a compliance checkbox. I treat it as a competitive advantage. When your metric definitions are trusted, your dbt logic is clean, and your pipelines are reliable — the whole organisation moves faster.

03

The best model is the one used

A beautiful attribution model sitting in a notebook is worth nothing. I build analytical frameworks that business teams actually adopt — which means obsessing over communication, change management, and decision integration, not just technical elegance.

04

Global perspective, local context

Having built analytics across 12+ markets in Europe and the US, I know that one size never fits all. Regulatory environments, customer behaviours, and data maturity vary enormously — successful analytics strategies are adaptive by design.

Background

Beyond the numbers

"My mission is to enhance business results through data-driven insights, collaborative effort, and analytical frameworks that actually get used."

I started my career as a programmer analyst — building dashboards with SQL and .Net for life sciences teams at Cognizant in Chennai. That engineering foundation gave me something most analysts lack: a genuine appreciation for how data systems work underneath the surface.

From there, I moved to London and spent a decade working across some of the most data-intensive industries in the world — from the real-time financial data infrastructure of Bloomberg to the hyper-competitive player analytics environment of Entain's global gaming brands.

I now lead the Data & Insights function at Punt, where I'm building the analytical foundations for a fast-growing iGaming business — owning everything from dbt pipelines and metric governance to VIP analytics and acquisition strategy.

I speak Hindi (native), English (full professional), and Tamil (professional working) — a reflection of a career built across cultures and geographies.
